plus Host game through the waiting room. The goal: explain the hand play-test reports and find what
blocks, confuses or looks broken. Read-only: nothing in the game was changed.

SceneTree scripts that have since been deleted. The runs were:

## The four reports

### 1. "There is STILL a mushroom attacking at the beginning. Is it supposed to be the specimen?"

**No enemy exists before the outbreak, on either path.**

- Run A, New game from the menu: 98 s from the yard to the training room with a census every
  stop. The result was `enemies=0 clouds=0 fires=0`, health 100, no damage events and no spawns
  (a01 to a14).
- Run C, the waiting room into the lab: 20 s in the yard after the level change, still 0 enemies.
- In the level file every one of the 43 spawners has `active = false`.
- The spore tanks (training room, grow room 0) hold shroom voxels, but they don't move (b01).

**The most likely thing the player saw is their own gun.** The first-person spore shooter
(`assets/vox/weapons/shroom_shooter.vox`) is a purple cap with glowing teal spots. A basic shroom
is a purple cap with glowing teal spots. The gun's glow voxels are tinted with the loaded ammo
color (`GearModels.build(..., ammo.color, ...)`, `weapon.gd`), and BASIC is teal, which is the
same teal as the shrooms. It fills the lower right quarter of the screen. It bobs, kicks on every
shot, and swings forward with the axe on melee.

The comparisons:

- b10 to b12: right after the pickup in grow room 0.
- b51: a real basic shroom standing next to the gun, nearly indistinguishable.
- c01: the waiting room, where the player holds it from the first frame, because the waiting
  room has no `start_unarmed` setting (see P0-3).
- Remote players carry the same model at head height.

**Other mushroom-shaped things in the first minutes:**

- The grow-bed shrooms in grow room 0 (b07). Static.
- The first real enemies. They spawn 10.7 s after the pickup, 8 m away and in view (run B log,
  spawner `s_0_grow_1`). That is by design.
- The outbreak also wakes `s_m1_hall1_1` and `s_m1_incub_1`. These are on floor -1, 4 m under
  the admin rooms, and they start trickling at once, because `wake_range` is a 40 m straight line
  (see P1-6).
  - The incubation spore shrooms sit under the lobby, break room and open office.
  - With the committed code, their 4 m release range and 4 m cloud blinded the player through
    the slab (`spore_shroom.gd` at HEAD, `cloud.gd` at HEAD). That is report 2, and the combat
    agent's working-tree change fixes it.

**Fix:** re-model or recolor the gun so it doesn't use the shroom palette, and shrink it or pull
it back (P1-1).
